A SMALLER UNIVERSE. NO SMALL QUESTIONS.

The waves disappear.
The mystery stays.

Take away one dimension of space and gravity loses its local waves. Yet boundaries carry states, black holes have entropy, and the quantum theory remains a remarkably demanding puzzle.

This is a route through the ideas that made the puzzle sharper. Read it in order, or begin with the surprise that catches your eye.
